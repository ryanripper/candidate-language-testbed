# Write-ups and article drafts

Every stage produced two documents: a **write-up** (the internal, numbers-dense
record of what was run and what it showed) and, for most stages, an **article
draft** (the same material written for a general technical reader).

These files live next to the analysis that produced them. This page is the index.

Each workstream also carries a dated `preregistration.md` stating its hypotheses,
metrics, and decision rules *before* the sealed truth was opened. Those are the
documents to read first if you want to check that a result was predicted rather
than found.

**Suggested reading order for a first visit:** the root README, then the
[synthesis write-up](../analyses/synthesis/synthesis-writeup.md) (which
consolidates every stage), then whichever workstream interests you.
